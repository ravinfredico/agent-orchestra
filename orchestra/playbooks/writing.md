---
id: writing
title: Writing
when: Draft or rewrite text (article, email, docs, story, plan) where structure and tone matter more than research.
---
## Steps
1. Outline: a writer with `files.write` writes `outline.md`: audience, goal, sections with one line
   each, target length.
2. Draft: the same writer (or a second one for long pieces, split by section) writes the file named
   in the task from the outline.
3. Edit: an editor with `files.read` and `files.write` tightens the draft: cuts filler, fixes tone,
   checks every section from the outline is there and the length is on target.

## Done-when templates
- outline: "outline.md lists the audience, goal, <n> sections and a target length"
- draft: "<file> follows outline.md, has every section, and is <min>–<max> words"
- edit: "<file> keeps every section, is within <min>–<max> words, and has no placeholder text"

## Pitfalls
- "Make it good" can't be checked. Turn taste into checks: length, sections, reading level, banned words.
- Give the editor the outline, so edits don't drop required sections.
